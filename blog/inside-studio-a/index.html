The heart of Heiga

Studio A is where most of our records start. A 16' × 20' live room with natural light, warm wood treatment and enough air for a full band — or the intimacy of a lone vocal take.

The room

High ceilings, controlled reflections and a piano that has appeared on more records than we can count. Every corner of the room was tuned by ear, not just by the book.

The gear

Our highlights include the Avid S6 console, Dynaudio monitoring and a Focusrite/Dante network tying the rooms together. Outboard favorites rotate through the rack — ask our engineers and you'll get a different answer every week.

Book a session

Want to hear the room for yourself? Get in touch — we'd love to show you around.
