A new home for our stories

Welcome to the new Heiga Studios blog. This is where we'll share session diaries, gear notes, award news and everything happening inside our Downtown Miami studio.

For years, our updates lived on a separate platform. Now they live here — in the same house as our music, our work and our team.

What you'll find here

  • Session stories — behind the scenes of records made at Heiga.
  • Gear & technique — notes from our engineers on recording and mixing.
  • Press & awards — milestones we're proud to share.

If you're an artist, a producer or just curious about how records get made, you're in the right place. Follow us on Instagram for daily updates, and check back here for the long-form stories.

— The Heiga team
